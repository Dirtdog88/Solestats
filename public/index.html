What AI tells runners to buy

Every month we ask three AI assistants the questions runners ask before buying, hundreds of times over, and score what comes back: who gets recommended, whether the facts are right, what gets cited, and where the sale goes.

Open data and method

Every prompt, every answer with its citations, every detected mention and route, and the scored table are free to download as CSV. How it works and what it can't tell you.

    SoleStats is an independent project. Scores describe AI answers on the dates sampled, not the products. Brands get their numbers a few days before each edition.
